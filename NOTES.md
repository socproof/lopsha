## Identity

**Aleksei Prokhorov** is the artist. **Lopsha** is the name the releases
currently carry on stores; a change to Aleksei Prokhorov is in progress with
the distributor. They are the same person — not collaborators. Any text that
credits "Aleksei Prokhorov" as a guest on a Lopsha release is wrong.

## Catalogue

Distributed by Too Lost. Two lines:

- **Electronic and ambient**, written and produced by the artist:
  Kaldt Skrekk, Grey Cathedral, Where Grief Goes Quiet, Nocturnal Static,
  A Million Gold, Pyatnashka's Yard.
- **Classical guitar**, music composed by **Evgeny Baev**, performed by the
  artist: Guitar Sonatas, The Malachite Casket, The Legend of Don Quixote,
  Retro Suite, Seven Dwarfs and One More, Concerto for Two Guitars and
  Orchestra. Baev was his teacher from 1995.

Collaborators, where they appear: Ilya Podoynitsyn (Music of Open Doors),
Gleb Chechin (Concerto), Artem Sergeichev (Crystal ship, 2010 — wrote and
read the texts).

Not everything is original: Music of Open Doors includes Villa-Lobos
("Etude No 8") and Domeniconi ("Koyunbaba IV").

## Details that keep getting guessed wrong

- The catalogue **is published**. 14 releases, most of them live since
  July–August 2026.
- Releases with Russian lyrics really do have lyrics — the language field is
  correct as it stands and should not be "fixed" to English.
- Track numbers that once appeared inside track titles on the classical
  releases were a delivery error and have been cleaned up.
